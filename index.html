Core Specialization

Penetration Testing & Enterprise Hardening

Moving beyond automated scanners to active adversary simulation—identifying critical exploit chains, quantifying real-world blast radius, and delivering board-level remediation playbooks.

The Business Pain Points

  • The Illusion of Safety: Automated vulnerability scanners detect known CVEs but completely miss multi-stage logic flaws and human privilege escalation.
  • Regulatory & Insurance Mandates: Cyber insurance underwriters, SOC 2, and NIST frameworks now require verified, human-led penetration test attestations to maintain active coverage.
  • Catastrophic Breach Latency: Attackers linger inside corporate infrastructure an average of 16 days before discovery; penetration testing identifies and cuts off dwell time before malicious detonation.

Why Penetration Testing is Done

  • Active Adversary Emulation: We test your environment using the exact tools, tactics, and procedures (TTPs) of modern nation-state and criminal threat groups.
  • Empirical Blast Radius Validation: We demonstrate what critical assets and customer data can truly be accessed, separating theoretical risk from operational impact.
  • Actionable Engineer-Ready Outcomes: You receive prioritized technical tickets for your IT engineers alongside concise executive summaries for your board and insurance brokers.
Stage 01 // Rapid Audit

Surface Perimeter Reconnaissance

Non-intrusive surface diagnostic interrogating your public digital perimeter in 60 seconds. Evaluates exposed DNS records, TLS cryptographic strength, HSTS headers, and external-facing assets with zero friction or downtime.

Controlled, full-scope penetration testing across web applications, cloud configurations, API endpoints, and internal networks. Emulates advanced threat actor techniques to expose privilege escalations and lateral movement.
