Change Healthcare: Remote Citrix Server Lacking MFA Yields $2.45B Ransomware Crisis

Healthcare • 192.7M Records • $2.45B - $3.09B Fallout
July Quarter Video Briefing (Digital Street Smarts)
Primary Vector & Root Cause:

Adversaries identified an internet-facing Citrix remote portal that lacked multi-factor authentication (MFA). Attackers utilized previously harvested credentials to establish an initial foothold, moved laterally across enterprise Active Directory in under 22 days, poisoned backup snapshots, and exfiltrated 192.7 million records before detonating the ransomware payload.
