Privacy Policy for Watz 4 Dinner

Effective Date: March 19, 2026

Leon R. Darden ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Watz 4 Dinner mobile application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. Information Collection and Use

To provide and improve our Service, we request certain permissions and utilize specific data processing tools. Watz 4 Dinner is designed to be highly privacy-centric.

  • Camera & Image Data: The Service requests access to your device's camera solely for the purpose of identifying food ingredients, pantry items, or grocery receipts. Images captured by the camera are securely transmitted to our AI processing partner (Google Generative AI) strictly to generate meal plans. We do not store, view, or share your images, nor do we use them for facial recognition or personal identification.
  • Text & Dietary Preferences: Any ingredients or dietary exclusions you manually type into the Service are processed to generate your meal plan. Your specific hardware blocklist (exclusions) is stored locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Service Providers

We may employ third-party companies and APIs to facilitate our Service and perform Service-related tasks.

  • Google Generative AI (Gemini): We use Google's APIs to analyze food images and text inputs to generate recipe JSON data. Data sent to Google is subject to Google's own strict privacy and data processing terms.
  • Firebase: We may use Google Firebase for anonymous authentication and backend infrastructure stability.

3. Data Storage and Security

The security of your data is important to us. Your dietary settings, exclusions, and generated PDFs are stored locally on your device. We do not maintain a remote database of your personal identity, email, or meal history. However, remember that no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our primary domain: leonrdarden.com
