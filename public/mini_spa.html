Anatomical Sector: The Eyes // External Perimeter & DNS Hygiene

OPTIMAL VITALITY

Clinical Diagnostic Context: The "Eyes" represent your external-facing edge surfaces—DNS boundary configurations, authoritative name-servers, public mail records (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), and perimeter IP allocations. Unmonitored edge assets represent the most frequent entrypoint for reconnaissance probes and automated exploitation bots.

DNSSEC Boundary Attestation: Valid & Synchronized
Dangling CNAME / Takeover Exposure: Zero Rogue Targets
Public MX / DMARC Reject Policy: p=reject configured

Therapeutic Note: Maintain automated weekly DNS zone diff tracking to prevent orphaned developer subdomains from becoming adversary staging points.

🩸

Anatomical Sector: The Arteries // Transport Encryption & Downgrade Protection

HARDENED CIPHERS

Clinical Diagnostic Context: The "Arteries" govern the confidential transit of customer data, session tokens, and business intelligence. Failure to enforce Perfect Forward Secrecy (PFS) or allowing legacy TLS 1.0/1.1 protocols leaves network pipelines vulnerable to adversarial eavesdropping, POODLE, and BEAST downgrade attacks.

Negotiated Protocol: TLS 1.3 (RFC 8446)
Strict-Transport-Security (HSTS): max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains; preload
Cipher Handshake Suite: TLS_AES_128_GCM_SHA256 (256-bit PFS)

Therapeutic Note: High-grade cryptographic hygiene verified. Protocol downgrade attempts are strictly rejected at edge load balancers.

🦴

Anatomical Sector: The Bones // Frame Protection & Structural Headers

ELEVATED ATTENTION

Clinical Diagnostic Context: The "Bones" form the architectural skeleton of browser rendering security. Missing modern framing constraints (such as Content-Security-Policy `frame-ancestors`) exposes user interfaces to transparent iframe clickjacking, cross-site scripting (XSS), and credential harvesting overlays.

X-Frame-Options Directive: DENY / SAMEORIGIN Active
X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff Enforced
CSP 'frame-ancestors' Alignment: Partial Baseline Detected

Clinical Prescription: Update reverse proxy or web server configuration to include explicit header: Content-Security-Policy: frame-ancestors 'none';.
