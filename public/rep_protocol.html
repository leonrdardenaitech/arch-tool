SUBJECT: Strategic Transition to Autonomous Security Operations via Ricochet Ephemeral Protocol™

1. Executive Context: The Crisis of Legacy SecOps

The modern Security Operations Center (SOC) has reached a point of systemic failure. As the average cost of a data breach climbs to $4.88M, our reliance on manual triage is no longer a viable defensive posture. Security analysts are currently paralyzed by a 99% false-positive rate, a condition driven by the Base-Rate Fallacy. Traditional detection relies on high-recall rules designed to capture every possible threat; however, when the base rate of genuine attacks is statistically minuscule—often representing only ~0.01% of daily alerts—these rules generate a noise volume that ensures critical signals are missed.

This "Crisis of Inertia" results in investigation backlogs and analyst burnout that leave the enterprise vulnerable. To secure the bottom line, we must pivot from reactive manual triage to the Ricochet Ephemeral Protocol—a framework that replaces human repetition with autonomous precision.
