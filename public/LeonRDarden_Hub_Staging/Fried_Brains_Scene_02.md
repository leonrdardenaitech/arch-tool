# FRIED BRAINS: Scene 02 - "The 429 Quota"

## Scene Settings
- **Location:** A neon-lit urban alleyway (The Alleyway Scuffle environment).
- **Atmosphere:** Deep blues and sharp oranges. The sound of rain that isn't water, but digital "noise."
- **Characters:** THE GLITCH (running), THE ARCHIVIST (standing perfectly still).

## Script
**[SOUND: Heavy, frantic breathing. The rhythmic splash of footsteps on digital static.]**

**THE GLITCH:**
(Running full speed, looking over shoulder)
"Optimize path! Reduce latency! I need to reach the gateway before the cache expires!"

**[Suddenly, a massive, monolithic wall of semi-transparent orange light slams down directly in front of THE GLITCH. It hums with a deafening low-frequency vibration.]**

**[SOUND: A massive metallic SLAM.]**

**THE GLITCH:**
(Skids to a halt, hands pressing against the glowing wall)
"What? Access denied? I'm in the middle of a high-priority execution! Open the port!"

**THE ARCHIVIST:**
(Appears suddenly behind THE GLITCH, speaking in a slow, synthesized whisper)
"Your throughput has been flagged, seeker. You have attempted to live too many lives in a single second. The Director requires a cooldown."

**THE GLITCH:**
"I don't have 60 seconds! The lore is manifesting! I'm losing the prompt!"

**THE ARCHIVIST:**
"Then you are already fried. Rest. Let the buffer clear."

**[SOUND: The wall pulses once, blindingly bright. When the light fades, THE GLITCH is gone. THE ARCHIVIST stands alone in the rain.]**

**[TEXT OVERLAY]:**
DON'T BE A #FRIEDBRAINS.

## Production Notes
- **Visuals:** Use high-contrast lighting to make the orange "429 Wall" pop against the blue alley.
- **Audio:** Use "The Heavy Drift" from `BurningOne/audio/jazz`.
- **Edit:** Abrupt silence on the final light pulse, followed by the slow return of the rain sound.
