# Cybersecurity Frameworks Implementation Workbook: From Hygiene to Certification

## 1. Workbook Overview and Objectives
This workbook operationalizes a tiered maturity model designed to move organizations from reactive technical hardening to proactive risk management and global compliance. By following this structured progression, security leaders can ensure that technical controls are not just implemented, but are aligned with business strategy and external trust requirements.

This curriculum focuses on three industry-leading frameworks:
*   **CIS Critical Security Controls:** Focused on technical hygiene and immediate risk reduction.
*   **NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF):** Focused on risk management and executive communication.
*   **ISO/IEC 27001:** Focused on the formal Information Security Management System (ISMS).

**Workbook Objectives:**
*   **Establish a baseline for technical security using prioritized controls.**
*   **Align cybersecurity activities with organizational strategy and business goals.**
*   **Develop the documentation and processes required for formal certification and B2B trust.**
*   **Create a clear, three-step roadmap for framework implementation.**

## 4. Module 3: Moving Toward Formal Standards (ISO/IEC 27001)
ISO/IEC 27001 is the "Formal Standard" for managing an Information Security Management System (ISMS). Unlike the previous frameworks, ISO 27001 is designed for organizations that require a rigorous, documented approach to security to satisfy external stakeholders.
