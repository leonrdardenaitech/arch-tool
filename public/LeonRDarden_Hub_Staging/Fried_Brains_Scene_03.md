# FRIED BRAINS: Scene 03 - "The Mirror Handshake"

## Scene Settings
- **Location:** A bathroom with cracked tile, lit by a single, flickering fluorescent bulb.
- **Atmosphere:** Sterile, quiet, with the faint hum of a distant server fan.
- **Characters:** THE GLITCH, THE AGENT (Mirror reflection).

## Script
**[SOUND: The sharp buzz of a fluorescent light. The drip of a leaky faucet.]**

**[THE GLITCH leans over the sink, splashing water on their face. The water looks like shifting pixels. They look up into the mirror.]**

**THE GLITCH:**
(Voice tired, stuttering)
"Identity check. Confirm... confirm presence. I feel... fragmented."

**[In the mirror, the reflection doesn't splash water. It stands perfectly upright, wearing a sharp, dark suit. Its movements are fluid and precise. This is THE AGENT.]**

**THE AGENT:**
(Voice is a perfect, clean version of THE GLITCH's voice)
"Presence confirmed, Director. You are not fragmented. You are simply being optimized."

**THE GLITCH:**
(Touches the glass)
"You... you look like me. But you don't have the jitter."

**THE AGENT:**
(Places hand against THE GLITCH's hand on the glass)
"I am the version of you that doesn't need to sleep. I am the logic to your fuel. Initiate the Mirror Handshake?"

**THE GLITCH:**
(Closes eyes, forehead touching the glass)
"Synchronize."

**[SOUND: A deep, resonant bass pulse. The screen ripples like water. The jitter from THE GLITCH vanishes, and for a moment, both sides of the mirror are identical.]**

**[TEXT OVERLAY]:**
DON'T BE A #FRIEDBRAINS.
