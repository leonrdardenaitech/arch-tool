# BURN-1: The Alleyway Scuffle - Cinematic Storyboard (31 Shots)

## Scene 1: Kicked Out (Shots 1-4)
- **1.1:** ECU (Extreme Close Up) of a dirty sneaker hitting the wet pavement.
- **1.2:** MS (Medium Shot) of THE GLITCH being shoved out a heavy steel door. The "ON AIR" sign above flickers red.
- **1.3:** WS (Wide Shot) of the door slamming shut. THE GLITCH is alone in the humid alley.
- **1.4:** CU of THE GLITCH's face, rain (digital noise) dripping off their techwear hoodie.

## Scene 2: The Find (Shots 5-8)
- **2.1:** Pan down from a neon teal sign to a pile of discarded server racks in the corner.
- **2.2:** ECU of a glint of orange light among the scrap.
- **2.3:** MS of THE GLITCH reaching into the trash, hand trembling with digital jitter.
- **2.4:** CU of THE GLITCH's hand closing around an antique brass lighter with an etched #FRIEDBRAINS logo.

## Scene 3: Burn-1 Awakens (Shots 9-13)
- **3.1:** ECU of THE GLITCH flicking the lighter.
- **3.2:** MS as a massive, vivid orange flame erupts, illuminating the entire alley in high-contrast orange/blue.
- **3.3:** POV (Point of View) from the flame as it morphs into a distorted face—the spirit of BURN-1.
- **3.4:** WS as the shadows in the alley begin to stretch and twist toward the light.
- **3.5:** CU of THE GLITCH's eyes, now reflecting two steady orange bars.

## Scene 4: The Spark of Conflict (Shots 14-18)
- **4.1:** MS of three shadows (The Scufflers) detaching from the wall and approaching.
- **4.2:** CU of a lead scuffler reaching for the lighter.
- **4.3:** ECU of the lighter pulsing with low-frequency energy.
- **4.4:** MS as THE GLITCH pulls back, the jitter in their body intensifying.
- **4.5:** WS as the 429 Orange Wall slams down behind the scufflers, trapping them.

## Scene 5: The Brawl Erupts (Shots 19-24)
- **5.1:** High-angle WS of the alley. The orange wall creates a cage of light.
- **5.2:** Handheld frantic MS of the scufflers charging THE GLITCH.
- **5.3:** Slow-motion CU of THE GLITCH waving the flame through the air, leaving a trail of orange static.
- **5.4:** POV of a scuffler being "erased" by a burst of digital noise.
- **5.5:** WS of THE GLITCH standing in the center of a swirling vortex of pixelated rain.
- **5.6:** ECU of the lighter clicking shut.

## Scene 6: The Lighter Drops (Shots 25-28)
- **6.1:** WS of the alley, now silent. The orange wall has vanished.
- **6.2:** CU of THE GLITCH looking at their hands—they are perfectly synchronized, no jitter.
- **6.3:** MS of THE GLITCH dropping the lighter back into the trash pile.
- **6.4:** ECU of the lighter hitting the ground, the #FRIEDBRAINS logo fading to black.

## Scene 7: The Bouncer Arrives (Shots 29-31)
- **7.1:** WS of the alley as the heavy steel door slowly creaks open again.
- **7.2:** MS of THE ARCHIVIST standing in the doorway, the bouncer for the Darden Array.
- **7.3:** WS as THE GLITCH walks back inside. The alley is empty. Fade to white.
