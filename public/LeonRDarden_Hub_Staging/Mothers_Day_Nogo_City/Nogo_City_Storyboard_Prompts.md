# ATLAS CORE: Nogo City (Mother's Day Project) - Starter Image Prompts

**AESTHETIC:** Jazz Tech, Cyberpunk Utopia, Vibrant Neon, Cinematic Lighting, Panoramic Wide Shots.
**RULE:** Keep backgrounds highly detailed for stable AI video panning.

### Scene 1: The Departure
**Prompt:** Cinematic wide shot, a dimly lit, weathered wooden dock at twilight. A group of casually dressed people are boarding a small ferry boat. In the far, distant background across a massive body of water, a breathtaking, hyper-advanced futuristic city glows with vibrant neon lights and towering skyscrapers. Contrast between the poor dock and the wealthy distant city, highly detailed, 16:9.

### Scene 2: Inside the Verse
**Prompt:** Interior shot of a boat cabin, warm ambient lighting. Passengers are conversing, relaxed and smiling. Through the large boat windows, the glowing lights of a massive futuristic metropolis reflect on the glass. Cozy atmosphere, jazz tech aesthetic, rich colors, realistic photography, 16:9.

### Scene 3: Approaching the Barrier
**Prompt:** Camera positioned behind the passengers, showing their backs. They are stepping out from the warm interior cabin onto the outdoor deck of the boat. Looming massively in the background is a vibrant, futuristic utopia city. Glowing water barriers and high-tech fences are visible in the water between the boat and the city. Flying cars zip between neon skyscrapers. Panoramic view, awe-inspiring, 16:9.

### Scene 4: The Jazz Tech Climax
**Prompt:** Camera strictly behind 5 people standing at the railing of a boat, their backs to the camera. They are holding up cameras and phones, taking pictures. The background is a massive, dazzling futuristic cityscape filling the horizon. Giant holographic digital billboards light up the night sky. Swarms of flying crafts leave light trails. Cyberpunk utopia, vibrant, bustling, masterpiece, 16:9.

### Scene 5: The Warning
**Prompt:** Camera strictly behind the passengers, backs to the camera. The boat has drifted too close to a glowing perimeter. Hovering aggressively above the water in front of the boat is a sleek, armed security drone. The drone is projecting a massive, glaring RED holographic warning sign across the water. The beautiful, vibrant city remains tantalizingly out of reach in the background behind the red glow. Tense cinematic lighting, 16:9.

### Scene 6: The Return
**Prompt:** A different, quiet dock at night. The passengers are deboarding the boat, facing the camera, looking satisfied and smiling, holding their cameras. In the far, distant background, the bright utopian city shines like a dream on the horizon. Peaceful resolution, cinematic depth of field, 16:9.
