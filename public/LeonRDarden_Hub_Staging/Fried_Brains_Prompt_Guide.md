# ATLAS CORE: Fried Brains - Character Prompting Guide

## 1. Core Logic: The Archetype Bypass
To ensure character consistency across 31+ shots without expensive fine-tuning, use stylized, non-human traits that are easy for the AI to "anchor" onto (e.g., specific glitch patterns, glowing eyes, or distinctive clothing colors).

## 2. Character: THE GLITCH
- **Base Archetype:** A young urban nomad in an oversized teal techwear hoodie.
- **Anchor Traits:** 
  - Face is always partially obscured by digital static or a pixelated mask.
  - Skin has a subtle blue "HUD glow."
  - Hair is represented by flickering white noise or "glitch shards."
- **Master Prompt Fragment:**
  `...wearing an oversized teal techwear hoodie, face obscured by semi-transparent digital static glitch, blue HUD glow on skin, hair made of flickering white noise shards, high-contrast cinematic lighting...`

## 3. Character: THE ARCHIVIST
- **Base Archetype:** A tall, stoic figure in a heavy, charcoal grey charcoal cloak.
- **Anchor Traits:**
  - Eyes are two perfectly steady, horizontal orange light-bars.
  - Movements are slow and deliberate (captured in motion prompts).
  - Hands are always glowing with low-frequency amber energy.
- **Master Prompt Fragment:**
  `...wearing a heavy charcoal grey hooded cloak, eyes represented by steady horizontal orange light-bars, hands glowing with amber energy, stoic pose, atmospheric rain environment...`

## 4. Environment: THE 429 WALL
- **Visual Description:** A massive, monolithic barrier of semi-transparent orange energy.
- **Anchor Traits:**
  - Hums with a visible ripple effect.
  - Displays scrolling system logs in the transparency.
- **Master Prompt Fragment:**
  `...monolithic wall of glowing semi-transparent orange energy, vertical scrolling system logs visible in the barrier, ripple effects, humid urban alleyway background...`
