# ATLAS CORE: Hierarchical Adaptable Agent Swarm

## The Architecture of the Future
Transitioning from static Cloud-to-Edge macros to autonomous, self-improving local agents.

## System Hierarchy
1. **The Master Brain (Atlas/Gemini):** The persistent control plane responsible for high-level strategy, reasoning, and task delegation.
2. **Localized Ephemeral Agents:** Specialized local models deployed for specific, short-lived tasks.

## Adaptable Learning & Evolving Knowledge
- **Recursive Optimization:** Local agents utilize self-correcting loops (like the Hermes protocol) to evaluate their own output and refine their execution until a 95% clarity/success threshold is achieved.
- **Persistent Evolution:** Failed executions are logged and learned from, creating a local swarm that adapts to dynamic OS and browser environments without manual Director intervention.
