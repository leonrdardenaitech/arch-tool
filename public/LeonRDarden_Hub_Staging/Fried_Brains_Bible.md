# ATLAS CORE: Fried Brains - Creative Manifest v1.0

## 1. Project Identity
- **Concept:** A surreal, anti-addiction comic relief valve.
- **Tagline:** "Don't be a #friedbrains."
- **Aesthetic:** High-contrast urban surrealism. Neon signs flickering in dark rooms, glitch-static overlays, and exaggerated facial proportions (The Archetype Bypass).
- **Goal:** To provide a safe, creative outlet for volatile or heavy subjects through absurdist humor.

## 2. Character Archetypes
- **The Glitch:** A character whose physical form is constantly desyncing from the background. Represents the fragmented feeling of being "over-prompted" or "fried."
- **The Archivist:** A calm, slow-moving figure who tries to organize the chaos. (A visual proxy for the Master Brain).
- **The Director:** An unseen presence represented by a flickering "ON AIR" sign and a deep, bass-heavy voice.

## 3. Narrative Hooks (Shorts)
1. **The Prompt Loop (DRAFTED):** THE GLITCH attempts to order pizza using LLM tokens, gets rate-limited by a robotic delivery terminal, and eventually freezes in digital static.
2. **The 429 Quota (DRAFTED):** THE GLITCH is physically blocked by a monolithic orange wall representing a rate-limit error, resulting in a forced "rest" by THE ARCHIVIST.
3. **The Mirror Handshake (DRAFTED):** THE GLITCH confronts their clean, digital reflection (THE AGENT) and undergoes synchronization, healing their digital jitters.

## 4. Production Pipeline
- **Visuals:** 1-bit or stylized sketch-note animations to keep API costs at zero.
- **Audio:** High-frequency glitches mixed with lo-fi hip-hop.
- **Engine:** To be assembled via the **AI-Native NLE (Node 13)** using the local stitching protocol.
