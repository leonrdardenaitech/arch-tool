Acoustic Sensing Protocol v1.5

WaVio transforms your microphone into a localized 19kHz ultrasonic radar field. Follow these steps to initialize the touchless interface.

STEP 01

Enable Hardware Permissions

Upon initialization, your browser will request Microphone Access. This is required to broadcast and listen for the 19kHz ultrasonic pulse. WaVio does not record or store audio data.

STEP 02

Establish Baseline

Place your device on a flat, stable surface. The system requires a quiet environment for the first 3 seconds to establish an acoustic baseline.

STEP 03

Hand Gesture: The Swipe

Perform a broad horizontal motion 4-6 inches directly over the microphone. The system detects the Doppler shift to trigger the Rewind (15s) command.

STEP 04

Physical Impact: The Bump

Deliver a firm tap or "bump" to the table surface near the device. The accelerometer detects the Z-axis force (> 8.5g) to Toggle Play/Pause.

Environment Recommendations

  • Optimal Distance: 4-10 inches from the microphone.
  • Surface: Solid wood or stone tables provide the best acoustic reflection and telemetry conduction.
  • Eco-Mode: If battery drops below 20%, high-fidelity visuals will swap to static placeholders to preserve power.
